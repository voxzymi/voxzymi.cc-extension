# voxzymi.cc

A Chrome extension that upgrades how your videos post to TikTok — better quality, fewer restrictions, cleaner metadata.

---

## What it does

**Post Bypass** — the master switch. When on, your uploads to TikTok Studio get pushed through in the highest possible quality instead of the compressed default.

**1080p60** — forces TikTok to accept and keep your videos at full 1080p60 quality instead of downgrading them.

**FPS Method** — quietly slips high-framerate videos past TikTok's fps limits so they post without being rejected or downscaled.

**Remove Topaz Metadata** — strips out the tags Topaz leaves behind, so nobody can tell your video was upscaled or enhanced.

**Checker** — paste any TikTok video URL and instantly see everything about it: quality, bitrate, resolution, sound, caption, download links for every stream, whether it's shadowbanned, and more.

**Studio badge** — a small `LIVE · voxzymi.cc` tag appears in the top-right of TikTok Studio so you always know the extension is on and working.

---

## How to install

1. Download this repository (green **Code** button → **Download ZIP**) and unzip it somewhere you won't move.
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the unzipped folder.
5. Pin the voxzymi.cc icon to your toolbar so it's easy to find.

Done. Open the popup and flip the switches you want on.

---

## How to use it

1. Click the extension icon.
2. Turn on **Post Bypass** (the master switch on the Home tab).
3. On the Settings tab, enable whichever upgrades you want (1080p60, FPS Method, Remove Topaz Metadata).
4. Go to TikTok Studio and upload your video like normal. You'll see the green `LIVE` badge in the corner — that means it's working.

For the checker, click the extension icon, hit **Open** next to Checker, paste a TikTok URL, and press Analyze.

---

## Updates

To update, download the latest ZIP, replace the folder, and hit the reload button on the extension in `chrome://extensions`.
